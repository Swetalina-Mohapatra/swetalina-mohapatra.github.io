Industrial Project Study

HR & Workforce Development • Research Case Study

A Study on Upskilling of Contract Workforce

Tata Steel Meramandali, Dhenkanal, Odisha

  • Training Infrastructure Audit: Evaluated existing training facilities, delivery methods, and workflows across departments to assess upskilling delivery for contract workers.
  • Skill Gap Identification: Conducted structured worker interviews, field observations, and workforce data reviews to evaluate baseline competencies and pinpoint training needs.
  • Workplace Barrier Analysis: Diagnosed operational hurdles impacting participation, including vernacular language gaps, varying literacy levels, low awareness, and vendor alignment challenges.
  • Worker Sentiment & Concerns: Assessed behavioral resistance and participation barriers, addressing fears over job security, lack of confidence, and limited awareness of training benefits.
  • Practical Interventions: Recommended multilingual materials, visual SOPs, micro-learning modules, and interactive sessions to boost engagement across diverse literacy backgrounds.
  • Strategic Outcome: Designed an inclusive, worker-centric framework that simplifies technical learning, increases accessibility, and fosters continuous upskilling.
